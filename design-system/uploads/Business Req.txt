####glossary and definitions:
Design:
A commercially sellable style with one design number. Might have different colors within one design style.

SKU:
A specific design + colour + size combination.

Production batch:
A planned quantity of one or more SKUs that moves through an ordered
process recipe.

WIP:
Inventory received from a process that has not yet completed the final
production step.

Finished goods:
Inventory received after the final configured process in the product recipe.

Jobber:
An external third party performing a production process such as dyeing,
embroidery, or stitching at a cost for us.####

####Photo-first UI requirements:

- Product photo must appear beside every design number in search results.
- Colour-specific photos must be shown wherever a colour is selected.
- Batch cards must show the primary product image. Primary product image should be settable and editable within the overall images for that product. 
- Material issues must show both material and destination-product images.
- Users must be able to search by design number, colour, category and tags.
- Images are supporting identifiers; database IDs remain the authoritative
  record keys.
- Samples and raw materials may exist without a final product photograph.####

####Production batch states:

Draft
→ Approved
→ Materials Pending
→ Ready for Issue
→ Process 1 Issued
→ Process 1 Partially Received
→ Process 1 Fully Received
→ Process 2 Issued
→ ...
→ Final Process Received
→ Finished Goods Verified
→ Closed

Rules:
- Partial issue and partial receipt are allowed.
- Received quantity may be lower than issued quantity.
- Rejection, process loss and excess receipt must be recorded separately.
- The next process may be issued only from available received WIP.
- The final process receipt creates finished-goods stock, not WIP stock.####
