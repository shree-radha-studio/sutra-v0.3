Sutra · flow maparchitect pack · 13/10/26

The five cross-module flows of the design board as strips of screens, one card per step, in the order a user meets them. Each card names the frame on the board, the role in the chair, what the screen reads and what it does, in the business's words. Dashed cards are steps that exist only as a button or are not drawn; they are findings, not omissions. Nothing here is a build instruction; the full tables are in screen-flows.md, data-on-screen.md and open-questions.md beside this page.
